# 2-Minute Demonstration Script — Aasan (आसन)

This walkthrough script is designed for live demonstrations, screen recordings, or product evaluation of Aasan.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### 0:00 – 0:20 | Introduction & Landing Experience
1. **Open the Homepage** (`http://localhost:5173/` or production URL).
2. **Point out the Brand Identity**:
   - The "Aasan" perspective seat logo in the top bar.
   - Dual-language toggle (`EN | नेपाली`) in top right — click **नेपाली** to demonstrate instant Nepali translation, then switch back to **EN**.
   - Clean hero section with the 3D TU Cricket Ground aerial banner.
3. **Featured Venues**:
   - Scroll down to highlight the two main venue categories: **TU International Cricket Ground** and **Cinema Halls** (QFX, Big Movies, CK Cinemas, FCube).

---

### 0:20 – 0:50 | 3D Stadium Exploration & Seat Sightline
1. **Click "View 3D Stadium"**:
   - The 3D stadium scene loads with the branded loader.
   - Show the stadium in **Night Mode** with 6 galvanized steel floodlight towers casting light on the pitch.
   - Toggle to **Day Mode** in the top bar: notice the galvanized steel masts, pale glass lamp lenses, realistic grass field, and soft cumulus clouds.
2. **Select Stand & Block**:
   - Hover over the stands: observe the hover card with pricing and availability.
   - Click **Grand Pavilion — Lower** (Block P1).
3. **Experience the Eye-Level Seat Fly-In**:
   - The camera swoops down smoothly from the aerial view into **Row A, Seat 1**.
   - Notice the pitch perspective, sightlines toward the pitch center, and the seat booking bar at the bottom:
     - Clear tier badge: `VIP`
     - Clean seat line: `Block P1 · Row A · Seat 1`
     - Prominent price: `NPR 1,200`
   - Click **Add seat** icon (`+`) to add to the cart.
   - Notice the sticky cart chip: `P1 · A1`.

---

### 0:50 – 1:15 | Cinema Experience & Multi-Hall Variation
1. **Navigate to Cinema**:
   - Click **Venues** in the header and pick **QFX Cinemas — Civil Mall (Audi 1)**.
2. **Showtime Selection & Ambience Glow**:
   - Notice the showtime picker pill in the header: `02:45 PM | 06:30 PM | 09:15 PM`.
   - Select `06:30 PM`.
   - Observe the ambient screen glow washing across the front rows, acoustic side panels, and carpeting with subtle brightness variations.
3. **Screen View & Best Available**:
   - Hover over central rows (Rows E–G) to see the floating screen sightline preview.
   - Click **"Best available (2 seats)"** to instantly grab prime contiguous seats.

---

### 1:15 – 1:40 | Buyer Details & Mock Checkout
1. **Click "Checkout"** in the bottom cart bar:
   - Notice the reservation timer hold countdown (`04:59`, `04:58`...) holding the seats.
2. **Buyer Details Form**:
   - Demonstrate inline validation: enter a single letter in Name, show error; enter valid name `Aarav Sharma`.
   - Enter Nepali mobile `9841234567`.
   - Check "Remember on this device" to demonstrate localStorage convenience.
3. **Payment Gateways**:
   - Click between **eSewa**, **Khalti**, **Fonepay**, and **IME Pay** to show distinct authentic branding.
   - Click **Pay with eSewa (NPR 2,400)**.
   - In the mock wallet modal, click **"Simulate Success"**.

---

### 1:40 – 2:00 | Digital Ticket & Exports
1. **Booking Confirmation Screen**:
   - Confetti burst!
   - Highlight the single-line monospace booking reference (`ASN-XXXXXX`) with one-click copy button.
   - Verify the demo honesty banner: *"DEMO — not valid for entry"*.
   - Point out individual QR codes for each seat with seat labels (`Grand Pavilion — Lower · Block P1 · Row A · Seat 1`).
2. **Export Downloads**:
   - Click **"Save Ticket PDF"**: opens or downloads a clean, printable vector A4 PDF with embedded Devanagari typography.
   - Click **"Save Ticket Image"**: exports a 1200px wide crisp PNG pass.
3. **Wrap-up**:
   - Emphasize zero-dependency client performance, full accessibility (2D SVG mode), and responsive design from 1440px desktop to 390px mobile.
