Brand Identity Design

Aasan (आसन) — Logo Exploration

Four distinct flat, geometric brand identity options for Nepal’s 3D seat-selection and ticketing product. Every symbol is optimized for pixel-clarity at 16 px (favicon), 64 px (app headers), and 256 px. All wordmark text is converted to vector path outlines.

Strengths

  • Directly communicates "Seat" (आसन) through unmistakable geometric backrest and armrests.
  • The radiating viewpoint dot introduces the 3D perspective / sightline concept without clutter.
  • Strong symmetrical geometry anchors nicely in square app icons and navbars.

Weaknesses

  • The subtle dotted line fades at 16 px, though the seat silhouette and vantage dot remain bold.
  • A direct chair representation is more literal than abstract.

Strengths

  • Clever conceptual synthesis of "Vision / 3D Seat View" + "Physical Seat".
  • High negative-space contrast inside the bucket seat reads with high punch at 16 px.
  • Modern tech-forward vibe that feels innovative and unique in ticketing.

Weaknesses

  • Needs careful line weighting so the eye motif doesn’t feel surveillance-like.
  • Slightly more complex cognitive load than a pure seat icon.

  • The crimson seat marker creates an intuitive "You are sitting right here" mental model.

Strengths

  • Deep cultural resonance for Nepal: built directly from the native letter "आ" (Aasan).
  • Unique typographic monogram that is impossible to confuse with global booking apps.
  • Very compact, punchy silhouette that renders razor-sharp even at 16 px.

Weaknesses

  • For non-Devanagari readers, it functions primarily as an abstract geometric glyph.
  • Requires the wordmark beside it for immediate Latin reading.
