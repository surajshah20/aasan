# Tribhuvan University (TU) International Cricket Ground — Design & Reference Notes

> **Source Priority:** User reference photos (`media_1795362590573_a8b63d00.jpg` night drone shot, `media_1795362590590_90cfa26e.jpg` stand photo) are the primary ground truth, supplemented by official stadium documentation and NPL renovation records.

---

## 1. Oval Proportions & Field Dimensions
- **Field Shape:** Cricket oval / ellipse oriented approximately North-South.
- **Dimensions:**
  - Overall field diameter: ~154 meters (major axis, North-South) × ~132 meters (minor axis, East-West).
  - Outfield boundary rope radius: ~65 m to 70 m from pitch center (long-on / long-off ~68 m, square boundary ~63 m).
  - Central Pitch Square: Standard cricket pitch strip measuring 22 yards (20.12 m) long × 3.05 m wide, oriented along the North-South bowling axis.
- **Orientation:**
  - **North End (Chobhar End):** Facing towards Chobhar / southern valley hills.
  - **South End (Pavilion End):** Houses the main Pavilion, players' dressing rooms, and VIP enclosure.

---

## 2. Number & Layout of Stands
The stadium bowl forms a continuous, closed stepped enclosure around the perimeter of the cricket oval:
1. **North Stand (Chobhar End / VIP Canopy):**
   - Central northern arc featuring the covered VIP canopy stand.
   - Flanked by open tiered concrete parapets.
2. **East Stand:**
   - Sweeping curved stepped concrete stand on the eastern side.
3. **West Stand:**
   - Sweeping curved stepped concrete stand on the western side.
4. **South Stand / Pavilion End:**
   - Houses the multi-story brick pavilion building with red gabled roofs, players' balconies, media centre, and VIP terrace seating.
5. **South-West Stand:**
   - Curved tiered stand connecting the Western stand with the Pavilion area.
6. **Aisles & Entrances:**
   - Radial concrete stairways (run between seating bays with silver handrails).
   - Vomitory tunnel entrances / pedestrian portals cutting through the bowl from the exterior ground level.
   - Continuous outer perimeter wall enclosing the bowl.

---

## 3. Tiers, Tier Heights & Rake Angles
- **Number of Tiers:** 2 distinct primary tiers:
  - **Lower Tier:** 6 to 8 stepped rows immediately rising from the field perimeter.
  - **Circulation Walkway / Concourse:** Wide horizontal concrete walkway (~2.0 m to 2.8 m wide) running between the lower and upper tiers for crowd movement.
  - **Upper Tier:** 6 to 10 stepped rows rising above the walkway.
- **Step Geometry:**
  - **Riser (Rise):** ~0.40 m per step.
  - **Tread (Run):** ~0.80 m per step.
  - **Rake Angle:** ~26.5°, providing clear unobstructed sightlines (C-value > 90 mm) over the heads of spectators in front directly to the pitch center.
- **Outer Wall:** Concrete perimeter wall rising ~1.5 m to 2.5 m above the highest top row to close the bowl.

---

## 4. Roof & Canopy Details
- **North VIP Canopy:**
  - Structural steel cantilevered truss roof covering the central rows of the North Stand.
  - Supported by vertical structural steel columns set behind the seating rake.
  - Dark slate/grey corrugated metal decking roof.
  - Front advertising fascia beam decorated in bold navy blue (`#003893`) and crimson red (`#DC143C`) with `#HAMROSHAAN` CAN/NPL branding (as seen in `media_1795362590590_90cfa26e.jpg`).
- **Pavilion Building Roof:**
  - Traditional pitched gabled roof with terracotta red tiles/sheets (`#881337` / `#DC2626`).
  - Covered viewing balcony for players and match officials.
- **East, West, and SW Stands:**
  - Open-air stepped concrete terraces with open skies.

---

## 5. Floodlight Positions (6-Tower High-Mast Array)
Installed for international day-night cricket:
- **Array:** Exactly 6 high-mast floodlight towers positioned outside the bowl perimeter in an elliptical distribution:
  1. **Tower 1 — North (12:00):** Behind the North Stand canopy.
  2. **Tower 2 — North-East (2:00):** Behind the East Stand.
  3. **Tower 3 — South-East (4:15):** Near the Pavilion and hospitality zone.
  4. **Tower 4 — South (6:00):** Behind the southern boundary entrance.
  5. **Tower 5 — South-West (8:00):** Behind the South-West stand.
  6. **Tower 6 — North-West (10:00):** Behind the West Stand.
- **Mast Specs:** 4 towers at 45 m, 2 towers at 60 m height; structural steel lattice pylons tapering upward to rectangular headframes carrying high-intensity white LED projector fixtures tilted inward towards the pitch.

## 6. Seat Colors & Distribution (Phase 2 Reference)
From the close-up stand photograph (`media_1795362590590_90cfa26e.jpg`):
- **Seat Type:** Polypropylene tip-up or bucket seats with contoured backrests anchored onto the concrete risers.
- **Color Blocks:**
  - **Sunshine Yellow (`#FACC15`)**: Dominant field color across the central blocks.
  - **Crimson Red (`#DC2626`)**: Alternating vertical section blocks and accent bands.
  - **Warm Orange (`#EA580C`)**: Secondary transition accent columns.
  - **Crisp White (`#F8FAFC`)**: Aisle-adjacent border seats and vertical division lines.
  - **Neutral Concrete Grey (`#64748B` / `#475569`)**: Concrete treads, risers, and radial stairways.

---

## 7. Pitch & Outfield Colors
- **Outfield:** Natural grass with subtle alternating mowing stripes (mown lawn green `#166534` and `#22C55E`).
- **Cricket Pitch Strip:** Standard 22-yard rectangular clay/loam strip (`#CA8A04` / `#B45309`) with crisp white bowling creases (popping crease, bowling crease, return crease) and wooden wickets.
- **Boundary:** White foam boundary rope encircling the field.
- **Sponsor Perimeter:** Low-profile LED / foam sponsor boards lining the perimeter just outside the boundary rope.
